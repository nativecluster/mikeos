MikeOS, the open-source legal AI platform by Open Legal Products, now runs on NativeCluster. Ask questions of your contracts and get answers that cite the page they come from, draft and redline Word documents with tracked changes, run tabular reviews across many documents at once, run workflows, and check documents against your playbooks. Every model call runs on your group's own Macs, and your documents stay on your own computer: each person's MikeOS keeps its own projects, chats and files, and NativeCluster's sign-in is the only one. A Word document's tracked changes that are not yet accepted are read as proposals, and MikeOS says which wording is the original. Not in this first version: sharing work between people, Google Drive, Gmail and Calendar, the Word add-in, and reading scanned PDFs (a PDF needs its text; a password-protected one needs its password removed first).
